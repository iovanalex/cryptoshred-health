# Scientific and editorial review

Revision date: 15 October 2026. Author-side critical review, not independent peer review.

## Assessment

The evidence supports a bounded conference case study. Its strongest finding
is disagreement between service state and a receipt or API representation,
repeated across three fresh deployments. It does not introduce a cryptographic
primitive, validated clinical intervention or broadly tested assurance framework.
The passing harness is not evidence that every application requirement is met.

## Changes applied

| Concern | Revision |
|---|---|
| The paper could read as a list of bugs | State the reusable claim-to-observation method and connect findings to five distinct assertions, without claiming priority or general validation. |
| Redaction language exceeded the automated checks | Identify the tested firstName, flags, named cache entry and KEK path. Exclude comprehensive field and copy coverage. |
| PROTECTED could imply a legal decision | Call it a prototype classification, record the default STANDARD_REQUEST reason, and limit the observation to the tested DOCTOR endpoint. |
| Independent assessment could imply independent investigators | Distinguish external HAPI software from evaluation by authors of the prototype. |
| SQL interpretation exceeded the observation window | State that SQL was inspected after failure and before GET. A SQL re-query after GET and later repair behaviour were not measured. |
| Repetition could imply reliability statistics | State that three deployments repeat one protocol; they do not estimate rates or constitute independent replication. |
| Scenario selection could appear blind or exhaustive | Disclose that source inspection guided normal-path and suspected-inconsistency selection. |
| Evidence could depend on expiring artifacts | Retain receipts and SQL outcomes supporting the revised claims alongside committed observations and validator outputs. |

## Publication boundaries

- RoEduNet: same prototype, different research question and new evidence. Cite
  the accepted performance manuscript; do not reproduce its benchmark tables.
- STC: brief conceptual link to source verification and escalation, without
  reproducing competency domains or claiming an educational intervention.
- Planned journal: historical recovery, key resurrection, tombstone
  reconciliation, scalability and proof evaluation remain outside this paper.
- Documentary fallback: preserved as a separate earlier analysis. It should not
  become another overlapping submission without a separate novelty review.

## Remaining limitations

The single-system, author-associated case and narrow fixtures are real limits.
No new run is required to support the narrowed claims. Generalisation, fixes
with before/after comparisons, consumer interpretation and clinical benefit
would require additional evidence. Update final proceedings metadata for the
accepted related papers when available; current references identify them as
accepted manuscripts. The Word file must remain within five pages after final
author edits. Submission remains a separate author decision.
